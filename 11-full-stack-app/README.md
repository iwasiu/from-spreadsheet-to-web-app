## 📝 What You'll Build

A complete business management system that combines:
- **Dashboard:** KPI cards, recent sales, low stock alerts
- **Customers:** Add, edit, delete, view customers
- **Sales:** Track transactions with customer and product data
- **Inventory:** Manage stock levels with low stock alerts
- **Reports:** Generate PDF reports (monthly, quarterly, yearly)
- **Email:** Send automated emails to customers

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Copy `Code.gs` into your Apps Script project
2. Create a new HTML file named `BusinessManagement.html`
3. Deploy as a web app
4. Click "Sample Data" to populate your sheets
5. Explore the full application!

## 📊 Data Structure

| Sheet | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| `Customers` | Customer ID, name, email, phone, company, status |
| `Sales` | Sale ID, customer ID, date, product, quantity, total |
| `Inventory` | Product ID, name, category, stock, price |
| `Settings` | Company name, currency, tax rate |
